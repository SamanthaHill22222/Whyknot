Cookie Policy

Last updated: June 2026

At YNOT (WhyKnot Wardrobe), we believe in transparency. This Cookie Policy explains how we use cookies and similar technologies to recognize you when you visit our website.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small data files that are placed on your computer or mobile device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work, or work more efficiently, as well as to provide reporting information.

How we use cookies

We use first-party and third-party cookies for several reasons:

  • Essential cookies: Required for the website to function (e.g., security and navigation).
  • Performance cookies: Help us understand how visitors interact with our site by providing information on areas visited and time spent.
  • Functionality cookies: Allow us to remember choices you make (like your preferences) to provide a more personalized experience.

How to control cookies

You have the right to decide whether to accept or reject cookies. You can set or amend your web browser controls to accept or refuse cookies. If you choose to reject cookies, you may still use our website, though your access to some functionality may be restricted.

Updates to this policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect, for example, changes to the cookies we use or for other operational, legal, or regulatory reasons.
